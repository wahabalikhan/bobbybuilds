## What changed

Tech Jobs used to pull in jobs from other job boards as well as from employers. That got the count to about 5,000, but a lot of those were the same role posted by different recruiters, or jobs that had already closed.

Now the board shows jobs straight from employers’ own careers pages by default. Other job boards are only used to discover companies. Jobs that only exist on other boards are hidden, behind an option to include them.

> A smaller board you can trust beats a bigger one you can’t.

## The trade-off

The job count dropped from about 5,000 to about 1,000 overnight. That’s a hard number to watch fall when you’re building in public. But every job left is real, current and has one apply link, on the employer’s own site.

## Where it is now

The number grows as the board finds more companies. Today it lists {open_jobs} jobs from {active_companies} companies, all straight from employers.
